How to Play

You control one player. When a loop ends, your movement is saved as a translucent ECHO that replays your exact actions forever.

Goal: Reach the green Exit portal. Some doors require multiple pressure plates held down simultaneously — something one body cannot do alone!

Controls: Arrow Keys / WASD to move. Press L to finish loop early. Press R to restart level.
